Set up Calendar

In Orc, open Calendar and connect a Google account or an iCal address. This vault reads your next 24 hours from it and builds 15 minutes before each meeting.

Notion Unchecked.
Set up Notion

Use the Claude login chosen in this vault’s Settings. Add Notion for that login.

claude mcp add --scope user --transport http notion https://mcp.notion.com/mcp

Open Claude Code, sign in to Notion with /mcp, then prep again.
