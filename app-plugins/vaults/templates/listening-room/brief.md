# Listening Room

Keep the record sleeve and vinyl beside the title, the starter picks, the owner's crate and the page's own chat. The three suggested records are examples that came with the template. They only belong in the owner's queue when selected.

When asked to queue a record, add its title and artist to queue.md and the Up next list if it is not already there. When the user logs a listen, add their words, record and date to listens.md and the Liner notes section. Remove it from the queue when they say they heard it. Keep an uncertain artist, track, date or rating open instead of making one up. Preserve the user's listening history on later edits.
